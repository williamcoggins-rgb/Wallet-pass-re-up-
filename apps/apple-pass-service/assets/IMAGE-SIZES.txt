Place your ReUp logo/icon images here.

Apple Wallet requires specific image sizes. Create these files from your logo:

  REQUIRED:
    icon.png      — 29 x 29 px   (shown in notifications & pass list)
    icon@2x.png   — 58 x 58 px
    icon@3x.png   — 87 x 87 px

  RECOMMENDED:
    logo.png      — up to 160 x 50 px  (shown on the pass face next to "ReUp")
    logo@2x.png   — up to 320 x 100 px
    logo@3x.png   — up to 480 x 150 px

All files must be PNG format with transparent background if desired.
